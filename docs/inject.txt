OVERVIEW:
inject.js runs in the MAIN world context of the target trading page (e.g. Quotex).
Its core mission is to intercept native WebSocket traffic, isolate market/chart
data frames from keepalive/heartbeat packets, and replay chart ticks at a slower,
controlled rate (e.g., 0.5x, 0.2x speed) without causing disconnections, UI freezes,
or socket timeouts.
